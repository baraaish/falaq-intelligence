## 9. Writing style

The final website copy should be:

- Direct.
- Clear.
- Practical.
- Specific.
- Professional.
- Understandable to non-technical buyers.
- Confident without exaggeration.
- Focused on operations and business value.

Avoid:

- Empty AI hype.
- Generic phrases such as “unlock the power of AI.”
- Excessive buzzwords.
- Fake urgency.
- Repetitive claims.
- Fabricated evidence.
- Fake customer testimonials.
- Fake client logos.
- Fake partnerships.
- Fake awards.
- Fake certifications.
- Fake performance results.

Do not invent facts about the company.

Pilot numbers may be used only when clearly described as targets, examples, or hypothetical scenarios.

Arabic and English copy should sound natural in each language. Do not use literal translation when it harms quality.

## 10. Medical-content limits

Medical solutions should be presented as:

- Operational systems.
- Administrative support.
- Workflow support.
- Review prioritization.
- Decision support.
- Quality-control assistance.

Do not claim that Falaq systems independently:

- Diagnose patients.
- Prescribe treatment.
- Approve clinical results.
- Replace qualified healthcare professionals.
- Make final medical decisions.

Qualified staff retain final responsibility where applicable.

Do not overuse warnings. State the role of human oversight clearly and professionally.
