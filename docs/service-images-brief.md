# Service images — download brief

**Where to save:** `assets/services/` (the folder is already created)
**Filename:** exactly the slug + `.jpg` — e.g. `lead-qualification.jpg`
Get the names right and every one of the 17 cards wires up automatically.

## Specification

| | |
|---|---|
| Source | **Unsplash** (unsplash.com) or **Pexels** (pexels.com) — both free for commercial use, no attribution required |
| Orientation | Landscape. Cards crop to 16:10 |
| Size | Download the **Medium / ~1920px** option. Do not download the original — it is often 20 MB+ |
| Format | JPG |

## Rules — these matter

- **No people.** No faces, no hands, no bodies, no silhouettes. This was your requirement and it also keeps the cards consistent.
- **Prefer dark or moody images.** The site background is near-black `#070708`. A bright white photo will punch a hole in the page.
- **Prefer objects, textures, screens, machinery, architecture** over staged office scenes.
- **Avoid** stock-photo clichés: handshakes, thumbs up, arrows going up, lightbulbs, wooden desks with a laptop and coffee.
- **Avoid** any readable brand name or logo in the shot.

If a search returns nothing usable, take the closest abstract or technical texture — a dark server rack, circuitry, cables, a control panel. Abstract beats a bad literal match.
