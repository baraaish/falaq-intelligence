About Falaq

We build AI that works inside business operations

Falaq designs, builds, and operates AI agents that connect business channels and systems to perform clear operational tasks, rather than stopping at answers inside a chat window.

Last updated: 16 July 2026
On this pageWhat we buildMarkets we serveHow we workContact
01

What we build

We build agents for lead qualification, quote follow-up, booking, customer service, accounts receivable, document processing, and CRM control. Every project starts with a real process that has defined inputs, decisions, and a measurable outcome.

  • Arabic and English conversational and execution agents
  • Integration with the channels and systems a company already uses
  • Clear permissions and human handoff for exceptions
  • A reviewable record of completed actions
02

Markets we serve

We focus on companies in Jordan and the Gulf, accounting for the language, operating context, and customer communication patterns of the region. Solutions are designed around each company’s systems and policies rather than forcing one generic workflow.

  • Jordan
  • Saudi Arabia
  • United Arab Emirates
  • Oman
  • Qatar
  • Kuwait
03

How we work

We start with one high-impact process, document the current state, define what the agent executes and what stays human, then build a scoped pilot before expanding. Success is tied to an operational result such as response time, data completeness, or reduced manual work, not message volume.

  • Process discovery and baseline
  • Permissions and exception design
  • Scoped build and testing
  • Gradual launch and monitoring
  • Continuous improvement based on data
