09 · Documents, Data & Systems

An answer for your staff, with the source it came from

Answers staff questions on policy, pricing and procedure from your internal documentation.

01

What it does

Staff handbooks, price lists, standard operating procedures, supplier terms and internal circulars are indexed and made searchable in plain language. A member of staff asks a question in Arabic or English and receives an answer that cites the source document and section, so that the answer can be verified against the original. Access follows your own permission structure: a branch employee and a finance manager are shown different material. You receive a monthly report of the topics most frequently asked, which indicates where documentation or training requires attention.

02

How it works, step by step

This is the real workflow with its rules and its limits — including the moment the agent stops and hands the case to a person.

  1. Arrives fromA staff question in Arabic or English
  2. Step 1Searches indexed handbooks, procedures, price lists and circulars
  3. Step 2Checks the asker's permission before showing any material
  4. ChecksDoes this employee's role allow access to this material?
    • PermittedAnswers and cites the document and section so it can be verified
    • Out of scopeThe material is not shown, and the question routes to the authorised owner
  6. RecordedA monthly report of the most-asked topics shows where documentation or training is needed

05

The guardrails it runs under

Four principles govern what we build and what we decline, and they apply to every agent without exception.

Operational honestyWe commit only to processes we can measure. Where an agent cannot outperform the current method of working, we say so before the contract is signed.
Human oversight by designEvery agent operates within defined limits and escalates to a named individual. Authority is granted deliberately by the client and never assumed by the system.
Arabic first, English equallyYour customers write in dialect, switch language mid-sentence, and expect to be understood. We build for that reality rather than translating around it.
Deliver, then expandWe begin with one high-impact process and earn the next. Scope discipline protects your budget as much as it protects our credibility.
